## Price encoding
`price = MDEntryPx (int64 mantissa) × 1e-9 × DisplayFactor`. ES DisplayFactor = 0.01, so
mantissa `614225000000000` → 614225 → **6142.25**. DisplayFactor normally comes from template 54,
which this capture lacks; 0.01 is the known ES value.

## Order events → L3 book
- Order events come from **two places**: the order group inside 46 (3,125 events, orders in the
  top 10 levels) and template 47 (931 events, orders outside the top 10). The L3 book must apply both.
- 46 order entries point to their price level via `ReferenceID` (1-based index into the MD entries
  of the same message): this gives price, side and SecurityID. All 3,125 resolved.
- Actions: 0 = New, 1 = Change, 2 = Delete. Queue order = `MDOrderPriority` (lower = earlier).
- 46 price-level entries are a ready-made **MBP-10 reference** for validating the L3 book.
- `MDPriceLevel = 10` appears 519 times: levels moving into / out of the top 10.

## Implications
1. Decoder works on real data; framing and sequence checks pass.
2. Without a snapshot, the book starts empty: orders resting before capture start are unknown until
   they trade, change or cancel. Validate L3 against the 46 MBP levels and track the unexplained quantity.
3. Overnight is too quiet for latency work (≈ 7 packets/s). Use RTH files (e.g. 2025-03-27 13:30 UTC).
